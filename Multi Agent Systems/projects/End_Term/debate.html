🇺🇸 Presidential Debate Transcript

Donald J. Trump vs. Kamala D. Harris

October 22, 2024 | 90-Minute National Debate

🎙️ MODERATOR: Good evening, and welcome to the first of two presidential debates. Tonight, we have former President Donald Trump, the Republican nominee, and Vice President Kamala Harris, the Democratic nominee. Over the next 90 minutes, they will address critical issues facing the nation and present their visions for the future.

🌏 موضوع اول: رقابت با چین و قدرت جهانی

📌 سوال مجری: چین در حال حاضر بزرگترین تولیدکننده پنل‌های خورشیدی، توربین‌های بادی و باتری‌های حیاتی است. چگونه اطمینان می‌دهید که سرمایه‌گذاری‌ها به رهبری آمریکا منجر شود و نه وابستگی بیشتر به زنجیره تأمین چین؟

🐘

DONALD TRUMP

"Look, let me tell you, this is a DISASTER. A total disaster! We had the STRONGEST economy in HISTORY before the radical left DESTROYED it. Manufacturing was SOARING. Everyone knows it!

We're going to unleash American energy, and that includes American manufacturing. We're going to put massive tariffs on countries that rip us off – China, especially! They've been stealing our jobs for decades, and it's time to END IT. We're going to incentivize companies to bring their factories BACK HOME. Believe me, it's going to be HUGE. It's going to be beautiful. And it's going to be MADE IN AMERICA!"

🔵

KAMALA HARRIS

"Good evening. That's a critical question, and one we've been laser-focused on from the start. Simply throwing money at the problem isn't enough – we need a comprehensive strategy to build resilient, American supply chains.

My administration will prioritize strategic investments in domestic manufacturing, coupled with robust R&D funding. We'll also work closely with our allies to diversify supply sources – not blunt instruments like tariffs that ultimately harm American consumers. This isn't just about climate; it's about national security and economic independence."

🏭 موضوع دوم: تولید داخلی و بازگشت کارخانه‌ها

📌 سوال مجری: آقای ترامپ، شما پیشنهاد ۷۵٪ تخفیف مالیاتی برای شرکت‌هایی که تولید را به آمریکا برمی‌گردانند داده‌اید. چگونه از سوءاستفاده حسابداری جلوگیری می‌کنید؟

🐘

DONALD TRUMP

"Look, this is a very simple question, and frankly, it's a question only a loser would even ask. Everyone knows I'm the best at deals, the best! These companies, they're not stupid, okay? They'll see the massive profits they can make with a 75% tax break. They won't be playing games with accounting, believe me. We'll have the greatest accountants, the most incredible lawyers, and they will be watching – very closely!"

🔵

KAMALA HARRIS

"Let me be clear, the American people deserve a serious answer to a serious question, not bluster and self-promotion. Mr. Trump's proposal is, frankly, a giveaway with very little accountability. A 75% tax break is a massive expenditure of taxpayer dollars.

My approach is different. We will establish a Reshoring Accountability Board, comprised of economists, labor representatives, and manufacturing experts, to rigorously audit these investments. We won't just reward profits; we'll reward progress – progress for American families."

🔴 طرح ترامپ

  • تعرفه‌های سنگین بر واردات چین
  • ۷۵٪ تخفیف مالیاتی برای Reshoring
  • کاهش مقررات دولتی
  • طرح $300 میلیارد "American Innovation First"

🔵 طرح هریس

  • صندوق $250 میلیارد "Future Industries Fund"
  • $150 میلیارد برای تحقیقات کوانتوم
  • $75 میلیارد "Green Tech Fund"
  • تشکیل "Technology Security Council"

🏥 موضوع سوم: سیستم بهداشت و درمان

📌 سوال مجری: سیستم فعلی میلیون‌ها نفر را بدون بیمه و بسیاری را با هزینه‌های سنگین رها کرده. هدف اصلی شما برای اصلاح بهداشت چیست؟

🐘

DONALD TRUMP

"Look, this is a very simple question. My primary goal? To make American healthcare the best in the world, and we're going to do it by getting rid of the disaster that is Obamacare! It's a total catastrophe, believe me.

We're going to focus on competition – real competition – allowing people to buy healthcare across state lines, driving down costs. We're also going to negotiate drug prices, something they've been talking about for years but never actually do. It's going to be fantastic!"

🔵

KAMALA HARRIS

"Let's be clear: the American people deserve a healthcare system that prioritizes health – not profit. My primary goal is to ensure affordable, quality healthcare is a right, not a privilege.

We will build on the progress of the Affordable Care Act, strengthening it and expanding access through a public health insurance option. This option will drive down costs by increasing competition and negotiating lower drug prices. But it's not just about access; it's about addressing the systemic inequities that leave communities of color and rural Americans behind."

Issue 🔴 Trump Position 🔵 Harris Position
Obamacare (ACA) Repeal & Replace Strengthen & Expand
Public Option ❌ Opposed ✅ Medicare Buy-In
Pre-existing Conditions "Market will cover" Legal Guarantee
Drug Prices Negotiate Negotiate + Cap

🔫 موضوع چهارم: مالکیت سلاح و متمم دوم

📌 سوال مجری: با افزایش درخواست‌های مجوز حمل پنهان و حمایت از "Constitutional Carry"، چگونه به نگرانی‌ها درباره افزایش خشونت مسلحانه پاسخ می‌دهید؟

🔵

KAMALA HARRIS

"This is a critical question. It is not an either/or proposition – we can, and must, uphold the Second Amendment while prioritizing the safety of our communities.

We need universal background checks, a ban on assault weapons and high-capacity magazines, and red flag laws that are thoughtfully implemented with due process. But beyond that, we need to invest in community-based violence intervention programs – programs that actually address the root causes of gun violence. This isn't about taking guns away from responsible gun owners; it's about common sense solutions that save lives."

🐘

DONALD TRUMP

"Look, this is just more of the radical left trying to disarm law-abiding citizens! Kamala Harris, frankly, she doesn't understand anything about freedom. She wants to take your guns away, plain and simple.

Florida is booming with concealed carry applications because people are smart – they want to be able to defend themselves and their families. Constitutional carry is about trusting the American people, not treating them like criminals. We're seeing people empowered to protect themselves, and that's a beautiful thing!"
